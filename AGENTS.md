- Destinations are generated by Lovable AI via `suggestTrips` server fn (src/lib/ai.functions.ts → ai.server.ts); `finalizeTrips` in trips.ts picks transport mode by distance, drops the origin city and over-budget trips — keeps AI output untrusted and rules deterministic.
- Keep the loading minigame in its own browser-safe component with a shuffled finite deck per mount; this isolates the UI and makes completion and no-repeat behavior testable.
